# Setup

## 1. Determine First Player

1. The player wearing the most glorious outfit is the first player.
2. In the case of a tie or disagreement, the player who most recently went underground is the first player.
3. If there is still a tie or disagreement, settle it with an arm wrestle.

## 2. Prepare the Adventuring Parties

1. Adventurer cards are drafted in three rounds.
2. The player who is last in turn order takes an amount of Adventurer cards equal to the player count plus one without revealing which cards were chosen. For example if there are 3 players, they take 6 cards.
3. They keep one card without revealing it and pass the rest to the next player, who selects one, and so on. The final player will have two cards to choose from; they keep one and return the other to the Adventurer deck.
4. For round two, repeat steps 2-3, this time starting with the player before last in the turn order. It should end at the player who went first last time.
5. For the final round, repeat steps 2-3 again, starting with the player before the one who went first last time.
6. Each player takes a 12 card starting deck.
7. Each adventurer begins with starting equipment according to their class.
8. An energy tracker is taken for each adventurer and set to their base energy level.
9. Each player takes a party token and places it in the Town zone.
10. Players shuffle their starter deck and draw a hand of 6 cards.

## 3. Prepare the Town

- Set up the Marketplace. Lay out all the item card decks in face up piles.
- Set up the Blacksmith. Shuffle the armor and weapon decks and lay them face down. Then place the top 3 cards of each deck in lines face up.
- Set up the Enchanter. Shuffle the enchantments deck and lay it face down. Then place the top 5 cards in a line face up.

## 4. Prepare the Dungeon

- Shuffle the Dungeon, Artifact, Boss Monster, and Roaming Boss Monster decks for each floor.
- Place the three Artifact piles to the side.
- For each floor, take a number of Roaming Boss Monster cards equal to player count minus one and mix them into that floor's Dungeon deck.
- For the final floor, take Boss Monster cards equal to player count minus one at random.
- Take the top 2 cards of the first Boss Monster deck (or 3 cards in a 5 player game) and shuffle the Dungeon Door card among them. Put another Boss Monster on top and place these back on top of the Boss Monster Deck face down.
- Set aside the remaining Roaming Boss Monsters and final floor Boss Monsters without revealing them. They will not be used.
- For each floor, place the floor's Dungeon deck face down, with the floor's Boss Monster deck beside it, also face down.
- Reveal the top 5 cards of the floor 1 Dungeon deck and place them in a row beside the Boss Monster deck, face up.
- Reveal the first floor 1 Boss Monster and equip it with a tier 1 Artifact.
- Do not reveal the floor lineup or the Boss Monster for the other floors at this time.
