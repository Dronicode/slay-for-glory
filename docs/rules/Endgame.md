# Endgame

## Endgame Trigger

The game ends immediately when the final boss of the final floor is defeated.

## Scoring

Players calculate their total Glory:

- Trophy tokens from bosses
- Glory printed on Dungeon cards
- Subtract Glory according to any Injury cards

The player with the most Glory wins and is crowned the most glorious person in the room.

In the case of a tie, the player who defeated the most Boss Monsters and Legendary Monsters wins. If there is still a tie, the player with the fewest Injury cards wins. If there is still a tie, nobody wins. Try harder next time.
