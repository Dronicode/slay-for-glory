# Gameplay

Players take turns in clockwise order beginning from the one chosen as the First Player during setup.

## Playing and Gaining Cards

When a card is played it immediately generates the Power or Wealth value written on it. Power is generated in the Dungeon and Wealth in the Town. Then, any effects the card has are triggered immediately. Some card effects have a cost or requirement. These only trigger if the conditions are met.

While in the Dungeon players can spend Power and Energy to slay monsters and overcome obstacles through Combat. Players can attack as many dungeon cards as they want if they have the Power and Energy for it. Defeated Dungeon cards become loot and other beneficial things once gained by a player.

While in the Town, players can spend Wealth to purchase cards from the Market, Blacksmith, and Enchanter.

Defeated Dungeon cards and purchased Market cards immediately enter the player's discard pile, while equipment and enchantments are immediately equipped by adventurers.

## The Deck and Discard Pile

Players all begin with identical decks of 12 starting cards. As they play, they gain new cards, which build a custom deck based on each player's strategy. The deck will often run out of cards and need to be refilled.

### Refilling the Deck

When the deck runs out of cards, refill it by shuffling the discard pile. Do not do this immediately when the deck runs out. Wait until cards from the deck are needed. For example, Player A's deck has 6 cards left and they must draw 6 cards, leaving it empty. Player A got all the cards they needed, so they do not refill the deck yet even though it is empty. Player B also needs to draw 6 cards but their deck has 4 left. That player draws what they can, then refills the deck, then draws the remaining 2 cards.

## Turn Sequence

Every turn plays out in four steps which are always completed in order:

1. Injuries
2. Travel
3. Action
4. Cleanup

### 1. Injuries

At the very start of a player's turn before doing anything else, they must play any injury cards in their hand. Some of these can affect later phases.

Some cards or skills have text which allows them to also be played during this phase.

If the player has no injuries, skip this phase.

### 2. Travel

A player may choose to travel between the Dungeon floors and the Town. They do this before playing any other cards. The travel options are:

#### Return to Town

Move directly to the Town from any dungeon floor.

#### Ascend the Dungeon

Return to any previous floor of the Dungeon, for example from floor 3 to floor 1, skipping floor 2. This can be done at no cost and with no penalties.

#### Delve Deeper

Descend to the next floor of the dungeon. Players can only gain access to deeper floors by slaying the Floor Bosses. If the passage card has been found, then a party may choose to delve deeper if they slew the boss of their current floor on the previous turn.

After leaving a deeper floor, the party must slay the Floor Boss again to return. Floors cannot be skipped, even if the player has been there before.

Example: A party on floor 3 of the dungeon travels to the Town to collect supplies. To return to floor 3, they must travel to floor 1 and defeat the Floor Boss. On the next turn they defeat the floor 2 Floor Boss. The turn after that, they can travel to floor 3 again.

### 3. Action Phase

The Action Phase is the main phase of a player's turn. The player may:

- Play cards from their hand
- Use Skills
- Trigger item effects
- Perform Dungeon actions (in the Dungeon)
- Perform Town actions (in the Town)

These actions may be performed in any order. A player may play some cards, attack a monster, use a Skill, play more cards, then attack another monster. A player may also choose to take no actions and pass to the Cleanup Phase.

### 4. Cleanup Phase

At the end of the turn, the cleanup phase prepares the table for the next turn.

- Any item cards played this turn are returned to their piles in the marketplace.
- The player discards all other cards played this turn, all cards gained, and any cards remaining in their hand. This does not count as playing the cards.
- Draw a new hand of 6 cards.
- Refill dungeon lineups:
   - To refill the dungeon lineup, first slide any cards in the lineup away from the Floor Boss toward slot 0 so they remain in the same order but with no empty slots between them. The empty slots should all be at the high end of the lineup, starting from slot 4.
   - Then draw cards to fill the empty slots from lowest to highest. New cards should always cost more energy than cards which have been there for longer.
- Refill the Town:
   - Fill empty slots in the Blacksmith and Enchanter displays from the appropriate decks.
