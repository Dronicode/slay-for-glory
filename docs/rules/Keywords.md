# Keywords

Use this page as a quick reference during play. Each keyword links to the section that defines it in detail.

- [Action Phase](Gameplay.md#3.%20Action%20Phase) - Main phase where you can play cards, use skills, and perform Town or Dungeon actions.
- [Adventurer](The%20Party.md#Adventurers) - A party member with class, Energy, Skill, and equipment slots.
- [Artifact](The%20Dungeon.md#Artifacts) - Unique equipment gained from Boss Monsters.
- [Blacksmith](The%20Town.md#Blacksmith) - Town display for armor and weapons, including upgrade and replacement rules.
- [Boss Monster](The%20Dungeon.md#The%20Boss%20Monster) - Floor guardian that unlocks deeper floors when defeated. 
- [Cleanup Phase](Gameplay.md#4.%20Cleanup%20Phase) - End-of-turn step for discarding, drawing, and refilling displays.
- [Cleared Floor](The%20Dungeon.md#Clearing%20Floors) - A floor whose deck and lineup are fully exhausted and empty.
- [Delve Deeper](Gameplay.md#Delve%20Deeper) - Travel option to descend to the next floor after earning access.
- [Display](The%20Town.md#Card%20Displays) - Face up cards available for purchase in Town areas.
- [Dungeon Card](Card%20Types.md#Dungeon%20Cards) - A Dungeon lineup card that can be fought and then gained as loot. See also [Attacking Monsters](The%20Dungeon.md#Attacking%20Monsters).
- [Energy](The%20Dungeon.md#Energy%20to%20Fight) - Resource spent by adventurers to join fights, based on lineup position.
- [Enchanter](The%20Town.md#Enchanter) - Town display for enchantments that attach to equipment.
- [Exhausted](The%20Dungeon.md#Last%20Stand) - State after pushing too hard in combat; removed by resting at the Inn. See also [Rest at the Inn](The%20Town.md#Rest%20at%20the%20Inn).
- [First Player](Setup.md#1.%20Determine%20First%20Player) - Starting player determined during setup.
- [Glory](Endgame.md#Scoring) - Victory points scored from tokens and Dungeon cards, reduced by injuries.
- [Hang Back](The%20Dungeon.md#Hang%20Back) - Option for an adventurer with low Energy to sit out a fight.
- [Injury](The%20Party.md#Injuries) - Negative cards or tokens gained from retaliation and other effects.
- [Last Stand](The%20Dungeon.md#Last%20Stand) - Risky option to fight with insufficient Energy at a Power penalty.
- [Lineup](The%20Dungeon.md#The%20Lineup) - Face up row of Dungeon cards on a floor, ordered by Energy cost. 
- [Marketplace](The%20Town.md#Marketplace) - Town area with single-use utility cards sold in static piles.
- [Power](Gameplay.md#Playing%20and%20Gaining%20Cards) - Combat value generated by cards and adventurers to defeat enemies. See also [Resolving Combat](The%20Dungeon.md#Resolving%20Combat).
- [Refilling](Gameplay.md#Refilling%20the%20Deck) - Rebuilding decks and displays when cards run out. See also [Refilling the Lineup](The%20Dungeon.md#Refilling%20the%20Lineup).
- [Retaliation](The%20Dungeon.md#Resolving%20Combat) - Monster counterattack value checked against party Defense.
- [Town](The%20Town.md) - Safe zone for buying, upgrading, enchanting, resting, and healing.
- [Travel](Gameplay.md#2.%20Travel) - Turn step used to move between Town and Dungeon floors.
- [Trophy Token](The%20Dungeon.md#Slaying%20a%20Boss%20Monster) - Reward from slaying Boss Monsters that adds Glory at endgame. See also [Scoring](Endgame.md#Scoring).
- [Upgrade](The%20Town.md#Blacksmith) - Buying higher-tier matching equipment to reduce cost and stack over old gear.
- [Wealth](The%20Town.md#Wealth%20and%20Cost) - Town currency used to purchase cards and services.
