# The Party

## Adventurers

Each player's party contains three adventurers of any combination of classes.

## Skills

All adventurers have a skill unique to their class, which can be used during the action phase of a turn.

- Some skills have special text allowing them to activate at other times.
- Some skills have the keyword "passive", meaning they are always active instead.
- Some skills have an associated cost to pay, or other conditions which must be met for activation.

Each adventurer can use their skill once per turn if the required conditions are met, but the whole party can act every turn.

## Starting Equipment

Every adventurer begins with some starting equipment. It is not powerful, but it gives them baseline Power and Armor for their first Dungeon runs.

## Injuries

Injuries are common when exploring the dungeon. They are graded by severity.

- Minor injuries heal on their own the first time they are played.
- Moderate injuries remain in the deck until healed by a Skill or Effect.
- Severe injuries can only be healed in the Town, for a cost.

Most injury cards are sustained by the party as a whole rather than by individual adventurers and go into the player's deck. Some have special text which causes them to be attached to a specific adventurer instead. These have ongoing effects but are otherwise healed as normal according to their tier.

The injury deck is finite, but there is no limit to the amount of injuries a party can sustain. If the injury deck is empty when the party sustains an injury, take an injury token instead. All injury tokens must be healed before any injury cards can be. Injury tokens count as moderate injuries for healing and scoring purposes.
