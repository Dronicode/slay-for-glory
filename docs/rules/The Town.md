# The Town

In the Town, players prepare for exploring the Dungeon. There are three areas with card lineups: Marketplace, Blacksmith, and Enchanter. The face up cards in each area are called a display, and players can purchase cards from as many displays as they want in the same turn if they have the resources to do so.

## Wealth and Cost

Wealth comes in three tiers of coin: copper, silver, and gold, where gold has the highest value and copper has the lowest value. The cost of each card in the Town shows the amount of each tier coin needed to purchase it. Each coin can be spent for the same or a lower value. Players can purchase as many cards as they can afford.

For example, a player with 5 copper and 2 silver could purchase a card costing 2 copper and 1 silver. They have 3 copper and 1 silver left. They can't purchase the card costing 2 silver because the copper can't count as silver, so instead they take a card costing 4 copper using their silver to count as the fourth copper.

## Card Displays

### Marketplace

The Marketplace offers cards with a wide range of uses and benefits which can be helpful or even essential during extended dungeon excursions.

- There are (undecided amount of) card types available at the Marketplace. Each type is visible in its own pile.
- If a pile runs out of cards it is considered sold out until a player uses and returns some of those cards.
- Marketplace cards are single-use, meaning after a player uses one, it is returned to the Marketplace display.
- Discarding Marketplace cards does not count as playing them and can be used to save them for later.
- These cards do not produce Wealth when played, instead they can be sold back to the Marketplace for their purchase cost - 1 of the highest tier coin. A card costing 2 silver and 1 gold would sell it back for 2 silver.

### Blacksmith

At the Blacksmith players can purchase new equipment.

- The Blacksmith has 2 face-down decks: armor and weapons.
- The display always has 3 armor and 3 weapon cards, so empty spaces are always refilled from the same deck.
- Purchased equipment is immediately equipped by an adventurer.
- Some Adventurer classes have restrictions on which Equipment types they can equip.
- If the adventurer already has equipment in that slot and they are different types, this is considered a replacement. The old equipment card can be moved and equipped on another adventurer.
- If the adventurer already has equipment in that slot and they are matching types (spear, sword, leather, etc), this is considered an upgrade. The new card is placed on top of the old card, and the purchase cost of the new one is reduced by -1 of the highest tier coin cost of the old one. For example, a new axe costing 2 silver and 1 gold upgrades an old axe which cost 3 copper and 2 silver. The highest value coin of the old axe was silver, so the new axe cost is reduced to 1 silver and 1 gold instead.
- The player can choose to pay the full cost and treat it as a replacement instead.
- If there is leftover equipment but there are no slots available, the leftover equipment is discarded.
- Discarded equipment goes to the bottom of the appropriate Blacksmith deck.

### Enchanter

At the Enchanter players can purchase enchantments to upgrade their equipment with.

- The display shows 5 enchantments.
- When purchased, immediately attach the enchantment to a piece of equipment.
- If the equipment is already enchanted, the old enchantment is discarded and replaced by the new one.
- Enchantments stay attached to the equipment if it moves to another adventurer but cannot be moved to another piece of equipment.
- When equipment is replaced, the enchantment remains on the old equipment.
- When equipment is upgraded, it keeps the enchantment.

## Special Actions

Aside from purchasing cards, there are some other actions a player can take while in the Town:

### Rest at the Inn

Cost: 3 silver

Effect: The adventurers will have +2 energy each if they enter the Dungeon on the next turn. If an adventurer was exhausted, they are no longer exhausted but also do not gain the energy bonus.

This action can only be used once and the effect does not stack.

### Visit the Healer

Cost: 2 copper / silver / gold

Effect: Heal one injury in the discard pile. Severity healed matches price paid: copper heals minor injuries, silver heals moderate injuries, and gold heals severe injuries. Injury tokens must be healed before injury cards.
