# The Dungeon

In the Dungeon, players explore and survive, slaying monsters for Glory and hunting for rare artifacts. Delving deeper into the Dungeon is the only way to gain the most points and to end the game. The Dungeon is divided into floors, with deeper floors being more dangerous to explore and also more lucrative. All Dungeon cards indicate which floor they belong to. Each floor of the Dungeon has 3 sections: the deck, Floor Boss deck, and the lineup.

## The Floor Deck

The Floor Deck represents unexplored areas of the Dungeon. As players explore the lineup and slay monsters there, empty spaces are refilled from the deck.

## The Boss Monster

Each floor has several Boss Monsters guarding the passages to the lower floors.

### Revealing Boss Monsters

The Boss Monster deck is always face down, located between the floor deck and the lineup. To reveal a new Boss Monster, flip the top card of the deck face up. The Boss Monster for floor 1 is revealed during setup, but the ones for other floors are not revealed until the first time a player enters that floor. Afterward, they are revealed each time one is defeated. Whenever a Boss Monster is revealed, it immediately equips a random Artifact of the same tier as the floor level. The monster uses that artifact as though it were an adventurer.

### Attacking Boss Monsters

Attacking the Boss Monster costs energy. The cost for each Boss Monster varies and is specified on the card.

### Slaying a Boss Monster

The greatest achievement a party of adventurers can hope for is to slay a Floor Boss Monster. When a Boss Monster is slain, the card is turned face down and placed at the bottom of the Boss Monster deck, not in the player's discard pile like other Dungeon cards. The player receives three rewards for slaying a Boss Monster:

- Trophy tokens equal to the amount specified on the monster card. These tokens are worth a significant amount of glory at the end of the game.
- The Artifact the monster was holding.
- Passage to deeper dungeon floors is blocked until the Passageway Card is found. When a Boss monster is slain and the next card is revealed, if it is the Passageway card this means the way to the next floor has been found. Set it aside and reveal the next Boss Monster card. This means that players may now travel to the next Dungeon floor. When a player travels to the next dungeon floor for the first time, if it is not the final floor mix the Passageway card into the Monster card pile for that floor before revealing the first Boss Monster.

### On the Final Floor

Boss Monsters on the final floor do not cycle until the floor is cleared like on other floors. There is a specific quantity of them and when one is slain, the card is removed from the game rather than returned to the bottom of the deck. Players still receive all the rewards for slaying it.

## The Lineup

Spaces in the lineup are numbered 0-4, with 4 closest to the Boss Monster, and 0 furthest away. The energy cost of each space matches its position.

The lineup for floor 1 is filled during setup, but for the other floors it remains empty until the first time a player enters that floor.

### Refilling the Lineup

Refill occurs only at the end of the turn, following these steps:

1. Cards in the lineup slide away from the floor deck towards slot 0 until there are no spaces between the cards, and all spaces are closest to the floor deck.
2. Refill empty slots from the floor deck.
3. New cards should always cost more energy than cards which have been there for longer.

### Clearing Floors

If a floor deck runs out and all the spaces in the lineup are empty, the floor is considered cleared. This is permanent and cannot be reversed.

- The Boss Monster for that floor is turned face down and cannot be attacked any more.
- Any cards that would be returned to that floor's deck are instead removed from the game.
- Players may skip cleared floors during Travel without spending energy or movement. For example if floor 1 is cleared, they may now Travel freely from the Town directly to floor 2.

The final floor cannot be cleared. If the final floor deck runs out, remaining bosses are not removed from the game. They must be defeated. When the final Boss Monster of the final Floor is defeated, the game ends immediately.

## Attacking Monsters

### Energy to Fight

Exploring the Dungeon is tiring, and the further the Adventurers go, the heavier the toll. When attacking a card in the lineup, each adventurer must spend the energy cost of that space to join the fight. Space 0 is free and costs no energy to attack. If an adventurer does not have enough Energy, they have two options: Hang Back or Last Stand.

#### Hang Back

The rest of the party join the fight, but the adventurer with insufficient energy does not. They hang back to catch their breath.

An adventurer who chooses to Hang Back does not contribute their Power and Armor to the party total, and cannot use their skills to aid in that fight. They do not spend any energy for this fight. Multiple adventurers may choose to Hang Back in the same fight, and this option can be used any number of times per turn.

#### Last Stand

The party is rushing to push forward and needs all hands for an important, dangerous fight. The adventurer with insufficient energy pushes past their limits to put up one final fight.

An adventurer who chooses to make a Last Stand spends their remaining energy to join the fight as normal but with a penalty: they suffer -1 Power per point of missing energy. If their Power reaches 0, they are forced to Hang Back instead. After the battle, they become exhausted and cannot fight again until they rest at the Inn in the Town.

### Resolving Combat

1. Calculate the party's total attack Power and Armor. This is the sum of each adventurer's base Power/Armor with equipment plus buffs from any played cards.
2. The monster retaliates against the attack. If the monster's Retaliation is GREATER than the party's Defense, suffer 1 Injury per excess point. Sustained injuries are effective immediately, including any effects on the party's Power.
3. Apply any monster abilities
4. If the total Power is EQUAL to OR GREATER than the monster's Defense the party wins and the monster is slain.
5. The defeated monster card is added to the player's discard pile.

## Artifacts

Artifacts are unique equipment that can only be obtained in the Dungeon. They are only gained by defeating Boss Monsters.

While equipped by a Boss Monster, it gains the benefits of the Artifact. Effects with costs can be activated as though the cost had been paid.

The player gains the artifact immediately after slaying the Boss Monster and equips it to an Adventurer. Each Adventurer may equip one artifact. If all slots are already occupied, the player may choose to keep their current artifacts and discard the new one, or discard an equipped Artifact to make space for the new one.

Discarded Artifacts are placed at the bottom of the appropriate artifact pile.

Before discarding the artifact, other players on the same floor may offer a trade for the Artifact. Any cards in hand or equipped artifacts are valid candidates for trading. The players can negotiate their own prices. Traded Dungeon cards and Marketplace cards are placed in the receiver's discard pile.

Players may never own duplicates of an artifact.
