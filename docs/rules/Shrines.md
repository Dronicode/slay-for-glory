# Shrines

Shrines are a type of non-threat card found in the Dungeon. They occupy spaces in the line-up but do not take or deal damage. Instead, they offer challenges for players to perform in order to receive special benefits.

## Revealing a Shrine
When a Shrine is revealed, take its Boon cards and place them underneath the Shrine card. In a 3-4 player game, use 5 Boon cards. For 5 players, include the 6th Boon card.

## Making an Offering
The only interaction players can have with Shrines is making an offering. Each Shrine demands a different offering, specified on its card. 

Each time a player makes an offering to a Shrine, they gain one Boon card from below it. Players can only make an offering to a Shrine once per turn, but there is no limit to how many times a player can return to a shrine to make offerings again in other turns to gain more Boon cards. 

## Boons

Each Boon is a reward from a strange ancient being with great power and grants a powerful bonus to the player who gains it. 

Boon cards remain in player's decks permanently and cannot be removed or destroyed by any means except the effects from another shrine.

When all the Boons from a Shrine have been received, the Shrine is removed from the game.
