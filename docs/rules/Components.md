# Components

## Party Components

- 15 Adventurer Cards
- 15 Energy trackers (Just use a bunch of dice or whatever)
- 5 Starting Decks (12 cards: 7 Attacks, 5 Fumbles)
- 5 Party tokens

## Town Components

- Market deck
- Weapon deck
- Armor deck
- Enchantment deck

## Dungeon Components

- Dungeon deck (one per floor)
- Boss Monster deck (one per floor)
- Artifact deck (one per floor)
- Injury deck
- Glory tokens (Dunno how many, just grab a handful of something glorious like glass beads or mini marshmallows)
