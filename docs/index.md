# Game Overview

Slay For Glory is a fantasy dungeon-delving deck-building game for 3-5 players.

Each player controls a party of adventurers, delving into a multi-floor dungeon to slay monsters and earn Glory. Players take turns, choosing at the start of each turn whether to explore the dungeon or visit the town. Exploring the dungeon and slaying monsters is the only way to gain Glory. Returning to the town is the only way to get better equipment for pushing further into the dungeon. At the end of each dungeon floor is a boss monster. Behind it is a new floor with even stronger monsters. The game ends when the final boss is slain. The player with the most Glory at the end of the game wins.

Who will build the most powerful party?  
Who will defeat the final floor boss?  
Who will emerge the most glorious?
